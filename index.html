How long does a ceramic coating last in St. George's desert climate?

A professional-grade ceramic coating applied by Lux Elite Auto Spa typically lasts 2–5 years depending on the coating tier and maintenance routine. St. George's intense UV exposure and desert dust make ceramic coating especially valuable — the hydrophobic, UV-resistant layer protects your paint from fading, oxidation, and embedded contaminants far better than wax alone.

What is paint correction and does my car need it?

Paint correction is a multi-step machine polishing process that removes surface defects like swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, and water spot etching. If your paint looks dull or shows spiderweb scratches under direct light, paint correction will restore a flawless, mirror-like finish. It is also recommended before ceramic coating to ensure a perfect bond.
